The ACM SIGMOD Anthology is a digital library for the database systems research community developed by ACM SIGMOD with the cooperation of the VLDB Endowment, the [IEEE Technical Committee on Data Engineering](http://tab.computer.org/tcde/), and the [EDBT Endowment](https://www.edbt.org/), and with the current assistance of many publishers and individuals, both in providing permission to include material here, and in locating physical copies to scan into digital form. The bibliographic information in the Anthology is integrated into the [DBLP Bibliography](https://dblp.org/).  [Michael Ley](http://dblp.org/pers/hd/l/Ley:Michael.html) is the founding editor and continuing inspiration for the Anthology. Michael created and maintains the outstanding [DBLP Bibliography](http://dblp.org). [Curtis Dyreson](https://dblp.org/pers/hd/d/Dyreson:Curtis_E=) produced volume 6 of the Anthology.
