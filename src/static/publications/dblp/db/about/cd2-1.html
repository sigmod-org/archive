This CD contains the scanned text of each paper from the International Conference on Conceptual Modeling (ER - Formerly: International Conference on the Entity Relationship Approach) until 1998 and from two ER'96 workshops. To locate a paper you may browse the tables of contents, use the Acroread full text index, or employ DBLP. To read/print the papers you have to install the Adobe Acrobat® Reader.
