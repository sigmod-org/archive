This CD contains the scanned text of papers from

  • The International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM),
  • The International Workshop on Data Warehousing and OLAP (DOLAP),
  • The ACM International Workshop on Advances in Geographic Information Systems,
  • The New Paradigms in Information Visualization and Manipulation (NPIV),
  • The 2nd Workshop on Web Information and Data Management (WIDM'99),
  • The ACM International Workshop on Data Engineering for Wireless and Mobile Access (MobiDE 1999,
  • The ACM SIGFIDET/SIGMOD Workshop on Data Description, Access and Control,
  • Databases for Business and Office Applications, Database Week, May 23-26, 1983,
  • The Workshop on Data Abstraction, Databases and Conceptual Modelling, Pingree Park, 1980, and
  • The Fifth Workshop on Computer Architecture for Non-Numeric Processing 1980
To locate a paper you may browse the tables of contents, use the Acroread full text index, or employ DBLP. To read/print the papers you have to install the Adobe Acrobat® Reader.
