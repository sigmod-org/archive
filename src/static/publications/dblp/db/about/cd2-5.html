This CD contains the scanned text of papers from

  • The Conference on Scientific and Statistical Database Management (SSDBM) 1981-1998,
  • The International Workshop on Database Programming Languages (DBPL),
  • The International Conference on Cooperative Information Systems (CoopIS) 1997 & 1998,
  • The International Workshop on Knowledge Representation meets Databases (KRDB) 1994-2000
  • The East-European Conference on Advances in Databases and Information Systems (ADBIS) 1994-1998,
  • The 1987 ACM SIGBDP-SIGCPR Conference,
  • The 1990 ACM SIGBDP Conference on Trends and Directions in Expert Systems, and
  • The SIGPLAN '76 Conference on Data: Abstraction, Definition and Structure.
To locate a paper you may browse the tables of contents, use the Acroread full text index, or employ DBLP. To read/print the papers you have to install the Adobe Acrobat® Reader.
