This CD contains the scanned text of
  • The ACM Transactions on Database Systems (TODS) 1996-1999,
  • The VLDB Journal 1992-1997,
  • Foundations of Databases by Serge Abiteboul, Richard Hull and Victor Vianu,
  • Concurrency Control and Recovery in Database Systems by Philip A. Bernstein, Vassos Hadzilacos and Nathan Goodman,
  • The Theory of Relational Databases by Dabid Maier,
  • The Benchmark Handbook for Database and Transaction Systems (2nd Edition) edited by Jim Gray,
  • Selected Papers from CACM - ACM Computing Surveys and POPL,
  • Notes on Data Base Operating Systems by Jim Gray,
  • SIGKDD Explorations Volume 1 (1999/2000), and
  • The IEEE Data Engineering Bulletin 1999

To locate a paper you may browse the tables of contents, use the Acroread full text index, or employ DBLP. To read/print the papers you have to install the Adobe Acrobat® Reader.
