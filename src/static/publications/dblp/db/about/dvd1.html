ACM SIGMOD Anthology is a digital library for the database systems research community developed by ACM SIGMOD in cooperation with the VLDB Endowment, the IEEE TC on Data Engineering, the EDBT Endowment, and with the assistance of many publishers and individuals, both in providing permission to include material here, and in locating physical copies to scan into digital form.
