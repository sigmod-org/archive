Collected Works of Jim Gray

Jim Gray is the Turing Award recipient 1998.

For seminal contributions to database and transaction processing research and technical leadership in system implementation.

Many works of Jim Gray were republished on Volume 1-4 of the ACM SIGMOD Anthology (click his name for a complete listing). CDROM 5-1 (and DVD 2) additionally contains some of his early papers:
