Welcome to the ACM SIGMOD Digital Review!

Digital Review provides a guide to topics of interest to the SIGMOD membership.  It helps visitors locate important works through an electronic review and editorial process.  Leading researchers from both academia and industry share their insights on notable papers.  See  article in the March 2000 issue of SIGMOD Record.

Digital Review encourages contributions from all members of the community,  so speak up!!
