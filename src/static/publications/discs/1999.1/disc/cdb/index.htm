WORKSHOP ON CONSTRAINT DATABASES (CDB'98)

in conjunction with ACM SIGMOD/PODS '98

May 31, 1998 Seattle, Washington, USA

The objective of this informal workshop was to bring together researchers to share and explore their views on constraint database technology and its applications. The main focus of the workshop was to communicate and discuss recent results of research into Constraint Databases and to identify future research directions.
