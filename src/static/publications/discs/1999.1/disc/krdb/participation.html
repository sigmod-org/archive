KRDB 1998

Participation Information




The workshop is open to all ACM SIGMOD/PODS'98 registrants, but you must send us a message at krdb98@ai.sri.com, to help us plan the workshop.

The KRDB'98 participants are required to register for ACM SIGMOD/PODS'98. The fee for KRDB'98 is included in the ACM SIGMOD/PODS'98 registration. There will be no separate registration fee for KRDB'98.
