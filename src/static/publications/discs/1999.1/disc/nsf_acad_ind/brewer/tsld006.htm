No DBMS...

  • Why not use a DBMS?

    • too slow
    • we choose availability over consistency
    • we need graceful degradation

  • Optimizations we make:

    • very simple updates: atomic table replacement
      • no isolation, no strong consistency
      • no nested transactions
      • essentially no locks (think atomic pointer swap)
    • OK to temporarily lose small random subsets of data due to faults (graceful degradation)
