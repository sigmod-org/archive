Trademarks

  • Protect indicia of origin of goods and services

  • Prevent against likelihood of confusion

  • Potentially infinite term

  • Independent adoption is not a defense

  • Civil and criminal liability

Notes:

    Trademarks are indicators of origin or source. If the mark applies to a good it is called a trademark. If it applies to a service it is called a service mark.
    Trademarks protect against a likelihood of confusion as to the source of a particular good or service.
    They can live forever
    Independent adoption is not a defense to trademark infringement
    Rights are enforceable in court by suit or by bringing charges if the infringement is willful.
