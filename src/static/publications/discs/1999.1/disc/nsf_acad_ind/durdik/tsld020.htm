Copyrights

  • Rights belong to the Creator

  • Exceptions

    • Employees - Work made for hire
    • Contractor Agreements - Contractors must agree to assign and assign copyright rights

Notes:

    Copyright rights belong to the authors of the work.
    With a big exception:
    1. If the employer hired the employee to make the work, then the employer may own the rights. This is called a ôwork made for hireö.
    2. Contractors do not fall within the work for hire doctrine. So, the employer must include a clause in the contractor agreement stating that the contractor agrees to assign her copyright rights to the employer.
