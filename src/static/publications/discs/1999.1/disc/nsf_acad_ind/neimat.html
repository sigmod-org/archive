Marie-Anne Neimat, TimesTenVP Engineering, TimesTen Performance Software.

Marie-Anne Neimat is founder and VP of Engineering at TimesTen Performance Software, a company dedicated to high-performance data management. Prior to founding TimesTen, she managed the Smallbase high-performance DBMS at HP Laboratories, and spun it off as an independent company in November 1996. The company released its first product in January 1998. She has done work in transaction management, and distributed data structures. www.timesten.com
