Cache consistency

  • Who should handle it?

    • All current solutions, except object-oriented DBMSs leave most burden to application:
      • application is conscious of cache
      • application decides when to refresh
    • Most caches are write-through
    • No coordination among multiple caches
    • Model does not scale (did not scale for OODBMSs, or for shared disk models)

  • How should it be handled?

    • Two-phase commit
    • Automatic (possibly delayed) propagation of updates
