Panos K. Chrysanthis University of Pittsburgh

    Current Research focus

  • Designing transaction processing systems in a variety of environments for network-centric data-based servers

    • which span reliable high speed WANs to low speed unreliable wireless networks
    • which include cluster of data servers to NUMA data servers
    • which support more flexibility & inconsistency management

  • Studying the suitability of existing popular protocols and designing extensions to make them scale.

  • Studying the effect of the network on the database performance and understanding the traffic requirements of database systems to efficiently use the QoS provisions in new networks.
