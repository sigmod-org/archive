SIGMOD Record is a quartely publication of the Special Interest Group on Management of Data (SIGMOD) of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM). The June issue each year is the Proceedings of the SIGMOD Conference. Contents of the current and past issues of the SIGMOD Record are made available on-line at :
http://www.acm.org/sigmod/record

SIGMOD Record contains regular columns including: Research Surveys, Systems and Prototypes, Research Centers, and Industry Perspectives. We also solicit contributions of articles, technical notes, reports, letters, and proposals for special sections. Conference announcements and calls for papers are publised if relevant to the interests of the group and, in most cases, are limited to one page. All contributions should be sent to the editor for consideration. Submitted technical papers are reviewed for importance and correctness. Priority is given to papers that deal with current issues of interest to a broad audience. Papers should be submitted electronically in either Postscript or Microsoft Word format to franklin@cs.umd.edu, and they should follow a format similar to that of the SIGMOD conference proceedings (but with a larger font):

  • point size 10
  • single-space, 2-column
  • 8.5in by 11in page size with 1in margins and no page numbers
Submitted articles are limited to 6 pages unless prior agreement of the editor is obtained. Hard copies will be accepted as an exception, in which case two copies are required, and page numbers should be written with pencil on the back of each page. Opinions expressed in articles and letters are those of the author(s) and do no necessarily epxress the opinions of the ACM or SIGMOD. Author(s) should be contacted for reprint authorization.

The SIGMOD Record (ISSN 0163-5808) subscription cost is included in the SIGMOD membership dues of $20.00 for ACM members, $23.00 for non-ACM members and $12.00 for students. The annual subscription cost for non-SIGMOD members is $27.00.

The web edition of the SIGMOD Record was established in September 1993. It contains the table of contents and all the articles (usually in postscript format) of each issue. Access to the articles is currently unrestricted, but, in the near future, access to new articles will be restricted only to SIGMOD members at first and the articles will be made available to everybody after a delay. The table of contents will remain publicly available all the time.
