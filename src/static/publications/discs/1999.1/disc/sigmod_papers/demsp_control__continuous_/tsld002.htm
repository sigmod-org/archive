Batch vs. On-Line Processing

    Batch Processing

    • Gives 100% accurate answers, but users must wait for entire query to finish . . .

    On-Line Processing

    • Gives progressively refining answers as the query runs!
    • Allow users to control processing.

    Applications of On-Line Processing

    • Large, ad-hoc queries in domains where approximate answers are acceptable (“big picture”)
