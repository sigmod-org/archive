Demonstrations/Applications for WHIRL
  • Computer games for children: 20,000 tuples, 2,000 review URLs, 17 sites, 55 extraction routines.
    On-line at http://whirl.research.att.com/cdroms/
  • Birds of North America: 200,000 tuples, 6,700 bird information URLs, 33 sites, 67 extraction routines.
    On-line at http://whirl.research.att.com/birds/
  • Movies (not available): eight sites, including IMDB and some sites that change frequently.

    In this demo, some information is collected at query time. (The other demos collect information off-line.)

  • Experimental data from several different domains.
