Highlights of the Joint Conference

PODS

Invited Talk by Ronald Fagin (IBM Almaden).

3 Tutorials on:

  • Overview of Query Optimization in Relational Sys.
  • External Memory Algorithms
  • Databases in Digital Libraries: Where Computer Science and Information Management Meet
Presentation of 28 papers in the Technical Program.
 
SIGMOD

Presentation of the SIGMOD Awards.

2 Invited Talks by:

  • Bill Gates (Microsoft Corporation)
  • David Patterson (UC, Berkeley)
Presentation of 42 papers in the Technical Program.

2 Panel Sessions on:

  • Are we working on the right problems?
  • Next Generation DBs won't work w/o semantics!
7 Industrial Sessions on:
  • The Next Generation of Data Access
  • DB Systems Management
  • Information Dissemination
  • Databases in Practice
  • Document Systems
  • Web Applications
  • Web Site Management and Applications
4 Tutorials on:
  • Electronic Commerce

Demos of 19 experimental database prototypes
