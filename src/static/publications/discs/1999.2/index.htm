DiSC CD#2 - SIGMOD Keynote Speakers
The Digital Symposium Collection is pleased to present the keynote addresses given at SIGMOD 1998 in Seattle, Washington. These are provided in MPEG format, and are accompanied by HTML slideshows which may be viewed in conjunction with the videos.

To view these videos, open /disc/video_gates.mpg and /disc/video_patterson.mpg from the root directory of this CD.
