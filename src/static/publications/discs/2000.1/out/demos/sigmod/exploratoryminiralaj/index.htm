Demonstration: A Prototype for the Exploratory Mining of Constrained Association Rules

Raymond T. Ng, Laks V.S. Lakshmanan, Jaiwei Han, Teresa Mah
SIGMOD 99, June 2, 1999


Welcome to a demonstration of our CAP prototype. The CAP prototype demonstrates a new exploratory model of association mining where the mining process is split up into two phases. In Phase I, users specify constraints on the data to be mined. In Phase II, users can manually select only those candidate pairs that are interesting enough to find relationships on, and proceed to select from three different relationship metrics to assess candidate association.

More detailed information regarding the exploratory model, the CAP algorithm, and this demonstration can be found in the handout given at SIGMOD 99.
