Atomicity with Incompatible Presumptions

Yousef J. Al-Houmaily and Panos K. Chrysanthis

  View Paper (PDF)  

Return to Transactions

Abstract
We identify one of the incompatibility problems associated with atomic commit protocols that prevents them from being used together and wei derive a correctness criterion that captures the correctness of their integration. We also present a new atomic commit protocol, called Presumed Any, that integrates the three commonly known two-phase commit protocols and prove its correctness.
