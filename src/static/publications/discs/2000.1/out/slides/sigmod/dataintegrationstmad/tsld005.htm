Construction of the Data Warehouse

  • The design of the Primary Data Warehouse is divided into sections by identifying “Facts” that are independent from each other and “Dimensions” that are common among section

  • Such a strategy allows for building Primary DW sections that:

    • Can grow autonomously and are integrated
    • Can be stored on different hardware

  • The set of all Integrated Primary DW section constitutes the Enterprise Data Warehouse
