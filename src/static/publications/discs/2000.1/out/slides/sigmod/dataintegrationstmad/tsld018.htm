Lessons (summary)

  • Integration is a step towards Warehousing
  • Construction of DWs is an incremental process
  • Methodology (and tools) can effectively support DW design
  • Impact on Information Technology is significant
  • A dedicated structure to manage DWs is needed
  • A good scheduling of the transformation task is critical
  • Clustering technology is effective on large volumes of data.
