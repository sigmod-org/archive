About TimesTen...

    TimesTen produces high-performance software that manages data in main memory.
    With TimesTen's In-Memory Database Technology, throughput is measured in tens of thousands of operations per second, and response times are measured in microseconds.
    Though internally unique, TimesTen’s products are accessed through standards-based interfaces, and designed for easy integration into existing software infrastructures.
    TimesTen products cater to consumer transactions that are at the heart of Internet Computing and of today’s Voice and Data Networks.
