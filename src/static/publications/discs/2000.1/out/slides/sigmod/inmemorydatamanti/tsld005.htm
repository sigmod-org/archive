In-Memory Data Management

  • Technology Premise:

    • If you can rely on the data ALWAYS being in memory… the number of INSTRUCTIONS required to perform data management operations can be dramatically reduced. It’s NOT a question of I/O - it’s CPU efficiency

  • The TimesTen Premise:

    • Across the full range of relational database operations, TimesTen completes, on average, ten times faster than a cached RDBMS. Some operations are more, some less.
