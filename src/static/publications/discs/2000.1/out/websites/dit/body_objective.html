Objective

The purpose of this workshop is to initiate and promote telecom data management as one of the core research areas in database research and to establish a strong connection between the Telecom and database research communities.

Topics

Topics to be addressed are those that focus on the application of database technology in telecommunication systems and services. Examples are:

  • Replicated and distributed databases in telecom networks: requirements and cases
  • Telecom database requirements
  • Data management in Telecom
  • network management (TMN)
  • data services
  • internet/WWW/Multimedia
  • Telecom database standards
  • Database support for IP based services
  • Databases in IN and TINA
  • Directory Services
  • Database support in Mobile networks (e.g. UMTS)
  • Real-time databases for traffic management,
  • Efficient storage techniques for call detail records
  • Database support for billing systems
  • ERP and Telecommunication
  • Database technology in service architectures

Form of the workshop

The workshop aims at a good mix between industrial experience and technical papers, as well as at a lot of interaction between participants. There will be an invited talk by Prof. Matthias Jarke (RWTH Aachen), presentation of accepted papers (with room for discussion) and a panel session moderated by Jim Gray (Microsoft BARC).
