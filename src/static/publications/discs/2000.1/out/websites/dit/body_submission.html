Paper submission

People that want to participate to the workshop are invited to submit papers on work in progress, papers on applications, as well as position papers. Papers should be at most 5000 words. Papers should be submitted electronically to w.jonker@research.kpn.com in one of the following formats: PostScript, MsWord, PDF, HTML or plain ascii. Accepted papers will be published in a workshop proceedings.

Deadline for submission: June 15, 1999

Notification of acceptance: July 15, 1999
