Objective

The Workshop on Research Issues on Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery (DMKD) was started three years ago as a forum to encourage database researchers to discuss and investigate data mining research issues focusing on large databases and data warehouses. The first two DMKD Workshops primarily focused on advances in data mining algorithms. DMKD '98 additionally emphasized data mining systems, applications and solutions. This year, as the field matures, we placed an additional focus on future directions.
