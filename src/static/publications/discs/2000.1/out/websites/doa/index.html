International Symposium on

Distributed Objects 
and Applications (DOA'99)

Edinburgh, Scotland

5th - 6th September 1999
 
 
 

The DOA'99 symposium is co-located with the VLDB (Very Large Database) 1999 International conference. The Proceedings of the DOA'99 symposium are distributed by IEEE Press. 

The DOA'99 symposium is supported by OMG (Object Management Group) and by the 
Interoperability Clearinghouse.

Best papers will be invited for expanded publication in a special issue  of the TAPOS International Journal in February 2000.

DOA'99 will be held at

Darwin Building, King's Buildings
University of Edinburgh
Mayfield Road, Edinburgh
EH9 3JZ, Scotland
