INVITATION and SOME POSSIBLE FUNDING from IFIP

ANNOUNCEMENT

INTERACT '99 is the Seventh International Conference on HUMAN-COMPUTER
INTERACTION (HCI) of IFIP TC13 (Technical Committee No. 13 of the
International Federation for Information Processing).

INTERACT '99 will be held in Edinburgh, Scotland, United Kingdom, 30th
August to 3rd September, 1999.

To obtain the Advance Programme and Call for Registration contact:

0511

email:  interact@meeting makers.co.uk  or web site http://www.bcs.org.uk/hci/i99

Some Funding Support Available

With financial assistance from a Developing Countries Support Committee
grant, IFIP is able to offer limited funding to assist some HCI scientists
from Asia, India, South America and Africa to attend INTERACT '99.  This
sponsorship will enable a total of about 5 to 7 scientists to attend
INTERACT '99.

Conference Costs

All scientists awarded one of the grants announced here will be personally
responsible for paying their full costs including travel to Edinburgh and
their conference registration fee.  This grant cannot be used to cover the
registration fee or costs of travelling to the United Kingdom.

To help you estimate the costs you will have to pay inside United Kingdom
some information is given here.  The special reduced conference fee, ONLY
available for scientists who receive this grant, is 250 GB Pounds (which
includes morning and afternoon refreshments and lunch for each of the three
conference days but not the conference dinner).

The cheapest accommodation is at Heriot-Watt University where the
conference is being held.  Approximate costs for the cheapest rooms are 28.50
GB Pounds per person in a single room with shared facilities, or 39 GB Pounds
for a single en-suite room.
Accommodation costs include a full Scottish breakfast.  Additional food
might cost about 12 GB Pounds per day.

Travel from Edinburgh Airport to the University would cost about 8 GB
Pounds by taxi each way, assuming that you will be using air travel to
arrive in the UK.

So the approximate total costs in Scotland to attend INTERACT '99 will be
about 470 GB Pounds to 575 GB Pounds.

If the UK entry airport from your country is not Edinburgh, the cost of
flying on to Edinburgh will depend on the airline you use.  This cost is
not included in the above estimate.  You will need to check this cost
yourself.

Payment of Grants

All grants will be paid in GB Pounds on arrival of the scientist in
Edinburgh. (It is too costly and often not possible to send payment safely
in advance).

Additional Benefit

Scientists receiving this grant will be able to participate in workshops
and/or tutorials at no cost.


APPLICATIONS

Applications are invited as soon as possible.  All applicants should send:

1.  a brief curriculum vitae, not more than one (A4) page in length

2.  a statement of how attendance at INTERACT '99 will benefit (a) your own
work, and also (b) your possibility of collaboration with colleagues in
developed countries, not more than one (A4) page in length.

3.  a statement of which TC13 working group workshop(s) and/or tutorial you
would like to attend at INTERACT '99

4 (optional).  if possible, one copy of your 'best' scientific paper in
English, or the name of a referee in USA, Europe or other developed country
who is familiar with your work

5.  the anticipated cost for you to travel to INTERACT '99 and the intended
route.

These statements must be written in English.

Applications will be received until the closing date of Friday, 2nd July.

REGISTER YOUR INTEREST IMMEDIATELY and send your application as soon as
possible, especially if you need to get a visa to enter the UK.

All applications must show the date of dispatch by post/ fax/ email.
