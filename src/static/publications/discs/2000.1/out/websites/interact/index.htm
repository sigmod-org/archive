INTERACT '99 took place in Edinburgh in Autumn 1999 - a major international conference presenting leading-edge research and development in almost all aspects of interactive computer systems and technology. INTERACT '99 was the last major HCI conference of the millennium.

The conference opened with a triumphant fanfare composed by Jim Alty and played by Glasgow Computing Brass featuring Jan O. Borchers (MPEG and Real Stream provided by Kari Hamnes and Silja Nyhus).


Fanfare for INTERACT 99.

The main pages on this site are accessible under four "tabs" at the top of this page:

  • The overview page contains a welcome to INTERACT 99 from the IPIP TC13 Chair,The British HCI Group Chair and the Conference Chair plus information on: The Conference Secretariat, The conference committees, and Background information on IFIP TC13.
  • The programme page gives an overview of the conference topics and a detailed overview of the timetable plus the whole programme in PDF format.
  • The venue page gives information on getting to Edinburgh and the conference centre plus links to other events in Edinburgh around the conference, HCI groups in Scotland, and Background information on Edinburgh and Scotland.
  • The registration page gives contact details for the conference secretariat plus information on the IFIP Developing Countries Support Committee grants which were available for INTERACT 99.
