IDM99 Milestones and Deadlines

  • Dec. 15, 1998, deadline for contacting all PIs, CoPIs, and speakers by email. Release the initial workshop web site and report template. First detailed budget plan review
  • Jan. 12, 1999, deadline for invited speakers and group coordinators to confirm participation
  • Feb. 1, 1999, deadline for the three-page reports of each project funded by IDM, even if none of its PIs attend the workshop.
  • Feb. 8, 1999, deadline for demo proposals.
  • Feb. 8, 1999, deadline for workshop registration.
  • Feb. 12, 1999, deadline for group formation.
  • Feb. 12, 1999, finalization of agenda and selection of demos
  • Feb. 15, printing of proceedings.
  • Mar. 7,8,9 1999, IDM99 workshop!!!
  • June 20, 1999, deadline for the release of IDM99 report to NSF.
