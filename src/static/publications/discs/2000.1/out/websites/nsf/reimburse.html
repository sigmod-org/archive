IDM 98 Workshop Reimbursement Information

For all PI's and selected attendees, you will be reimbursed for attendance at the meeting.

Participants will be reimbursed for the cost of hotel rooms for Sunday and Monday, as well as Saturday for those who get a cheaper rate with a Saturday night stay.

NOTE: The cost of a rental car is not covered

In addition NSF will reimburse up to $42 per day, which breaks down to:
Breakfast $9.00
Lunch $9.00
Dinner $22.00
Incidentals $2.00

Reimbursement Instructions

If your last name starts with a letter in the range of A to C please seek reimbursement from the University of Pittsburgh.

If your last name starts with a letter in the range of D to Z please seek reimbursement from Columbia University.
