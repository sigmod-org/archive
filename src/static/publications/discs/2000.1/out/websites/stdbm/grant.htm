TRAVEL GRANTS AVAILABLE FOR STDBM'99
(APPLICATIONS NO LONGER POSSIBLE)

We are pleased to announce that the steering committee of the 1995 International Workshop on Temporal Databases, which was held with VLDB'95, has chosen to make it profits available for travel grants for students attending the 1999 Workshop on Spatio-Temporal Database Management, STDBM'99, co-located with VLDB'99. Specifically, a small number of travel grants are available to students that register for STDBM'99. The grants will cover or partially cover documented travel expenses, but not other expenses. The grants will be given on a first-come-first-served basis.

Please contact us at Michel.Scholl@inria.fr and csj@cs.auc.dk if you would like to be taken into consideration for a grant.
Students affiliated with Aalborg University or INRIA are not eligible.

Christian S. Jensen and Michel Scholl

Program Chairs
