Exhibition Costs and Facilities

The VLDB Exhibition is based around a standard exhibit space of 3 metres by 3 metres, or 9 square metres, larger exhibits may be possible with costs subject to individual negotiation. Costs are based on an exhibit being set up on Monday 6th September and broken down on Friday 10th September. There are two levels of cost, based on the facilities provided by the EICC for the exhibit, and these are :

Space Only - (9 square metres [net] = 13.5 square metres [gross])

Furniture
 1 x 4' Burgess Table
  2 x Chairs
Electrics
 13 amp supply
Cost
 £600 + VAT

Shell Scheme - (9 square metres [net] = 13.5 square metres [gross])

Shell Scheme
 Standard Shell including 2 spotlights and 1 x 500W socket
Furniture
 1 x 4' Burgess Table
  2 x Chairs
Electrics
 13 amp supply
Cost
 £1200 + VAT

Additional, furniture, telecommunications, catering and flowers can also be arranged through EICC, these arrangements can be made direct once an exhibit has been booked.

The EICC has set out the rules and regulations governing exhibitions at their site, these are described in the Terms and Conditions for Exhibition.

Should you require further information, or wish to book an Exhibition stand, please contact the Exhibition Organiser,  Lachlan MacKinnon .
