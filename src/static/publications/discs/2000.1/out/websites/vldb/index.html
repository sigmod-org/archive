Organisation of Exhibition

The VLDB Exhibition will be held in the Lomond Suite at the rear of the main entrance hall of the EICC. This area will be the location for delegate lunches and tea and coffee breaks to encourage delegate circulation around the exhibits. (The talks will take place in the Pentland Suite on the upper levels.)

The Exhibition is intended to provide a forum for software vendors and developers, industrial solution providers, and publishers. The audience of conference delegates, which will be a worldwide audience in excess of 500 delegates, will be augmented by local industrial and commercial visitors attending on both a daily and full conference basis.

A CAD plan of the current layout of the Exhibition space, indicating provisional location of stands, is provided here.
