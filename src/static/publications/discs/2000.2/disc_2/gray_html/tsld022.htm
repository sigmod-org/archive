The Turing Test

  • Imitation Game:

    • Judge, man, and a woman
    • All chat via Email.
    • Man pretends to be a woman.
    • Man lies, woman tries to help judge.
    • Judge must identify man after 5 minutes.

  • Turing Test

    • Replace man or woman with a computer.
    • Fool judge 30% of the time.
