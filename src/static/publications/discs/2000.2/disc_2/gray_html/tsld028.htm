Prosthetics: 3 more challenges

  • Implicit in the Turing Test:

      Read and understand as well as a human

      Think and write as well as a human

  • Hear as well as a person (native speaker): speech to text

  • Speak as well as a person (native speaker): text to speech

  • See as well as a person (recognize objects and behavior).

  • Illustrate as well as a person (done!) but virtual reality is still a major challenge. create realistic 3D scenes in real time

  • Remember what is seen and heard and quickly return it on request.
