Benefits of Prosthetics

  • Today:

    • computers read for the blind (OCR &text to speech)
    • Hear for the deaf (speech to text)
    • Type for the impaired (speech to text).

  • Soon:

    • Prosthetics for all of us (better vision, hearing, memory, …)
    • Communication tools
      • Translating telephones ...
    • Revolutionize the human-computer interface.
