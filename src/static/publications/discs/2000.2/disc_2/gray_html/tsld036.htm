The Librarian Find and Summarize Information

  • Build a system that, given a text corpus, can answer questions about the text and summarize it

    • As precisely as a human expert in that field.
    • As quickly as a human expert in that field.

    Do the same for:

    • Sounds: conversations, music
    • Images: pictures, art, movies,…

  • Note: This will be a multi-media interface: vision, speech, gestures, graphics,... (not just language)
