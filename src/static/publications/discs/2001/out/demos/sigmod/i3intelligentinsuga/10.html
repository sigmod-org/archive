Another feature of the frontend, is an information-bar that displays the total information in the parts of the cube visited so far. For instance, from the five one-dimensional views of the cube in the previous screen the user has acquired approximately 50% of the information content of the cube as shown by the half-filled status bar. For the remaining 50% the user needs to dig deeper. Depending on the user's interest, he could either continue with this guided drilling of data or ask for a summary of the remaining 50% of the information.
