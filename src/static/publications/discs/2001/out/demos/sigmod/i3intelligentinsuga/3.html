In the adjoining slides we will show screen shots from the frontend of our prototype. This is a typical OLAP frontend with special extensions to support our new operators. In the first view we show the total revenue in millions of dollars for different geographies from 1990 to 1994. The revenue has been aggregated over all products and platforms. Notice that the revenue shows a healthy increase from 1990 to 1994 in most cases. One exception is "Rest of World" where we notice a drop from 1990 to 1991. The starting step in trying to understand why this drop occurred is to manually dig into detailed revenues of different Products and Platforms and find the specific cases that contributed to the drop. This could be a rather time consuming process if done manually. We illustrate in the next slide how the "DIFF" operator can be used to automate this step.
