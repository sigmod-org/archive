We show another example, this time involving an increase in revenue. On the screen, we see the total revenue for different categories in the Product group 'Soln'. The user wants to understand the reason for the sudden increase in revenue of ``Vertical Apps'' from 2826 in 1992 to 7947 in 1993 --- almost a factor of three increase. For the answer he can again invoke the DIFF operator. The result returned is a compact summary of the reason for the increase. The first row of the answer, shows that overall the revenue increased by a modest 16\% after discounting the rows underneath it. The next set of rows indicate that the main increase is due to product categories Manufacturing-Process, Other vertical Apps, Manufacturing-Discrete and Health care in various geographies and platforms. In this case we have achieved a significant reduction in answer size by summarizing over all different platforms and geographies with the same change.

This example shows how we could summarize detailed values with similar changes into a single row.
