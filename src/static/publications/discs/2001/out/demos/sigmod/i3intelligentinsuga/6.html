We next demonstrate another operator called the "Relax" operator. This presents another example of automating the existing manual discovery process. The Relax operator is a symmetric counterpart of the DIFF operator. The DIFF operator enables us to dig deeper into a problem case by focussing the drill-down effort to the detailed problem cases. The Relax operator on the other hand, starts from a problem case at the detailed level and rolls up to generalize the problem case as far as possible. Consider an example next.

Suppose an analyst observes the big drop in revenue from 1993 to 1994 for Product = ``HRM/Payroll'', Geography = ``United States'' and Platform = ``Single-User Other''. He might be able to diagnose the reason better if he found the most general scope of the problem. Was this problem specific to "United States" or other regions had similar drops? Was there something wrong with the Platform? He searches for all these answers by doing a sequence of roll ups, selections, pivots etc around the problem case. We automate these operations using the "Relax" operator.
