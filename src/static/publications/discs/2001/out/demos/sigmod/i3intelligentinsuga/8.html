We next show the working of the inform operator. The goal of this operator is to help a user concentrate on the most informative parts of the cube. The tool keeps track of the parts of the cube that the user has already visited and uses that information to guide him to the most interesting unvisited parts.
We illustrate the working of this operator next with the example of another dataset obtained from the enrollment statistics of a major university. The data contains the total number of registered students in each department for each year in the last decade divided into the degree program and the category and gender of the student. The values within brackets is the number of distinct members at that level of hierachy.
