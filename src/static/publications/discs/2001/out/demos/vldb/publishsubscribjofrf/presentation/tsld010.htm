Subscription Language

  • A subscription is a conjunction of predicates.

  • Predicate: <attribute, operator, value>

    • = ,>, <
    • contains for multi-valued attributes.
    • is-kind-of for hierarchical domains.

    Subscription: (topic contains generic_auction) AND
    (topic contains Antiques) AND
    (price < 10000) AND
    (style = Louis XV)
