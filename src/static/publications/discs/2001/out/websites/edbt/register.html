Register for EDBT 2000

Please note that the online registration for EDBT 2000 has been closed
You are, however, welcome to attend the conference anyway: simply register on-site.

Secure Online Registration (via the Web)
Supply your data by completing this secure online form.

The form is hosted by a secure http server; the connection between this server and your web client will be encrypted if your client supports https/SSL - most recent clients do. Your client may not recognize the authority that created the authentification certifcate for this host but the transmission will be encrypted anyway.
