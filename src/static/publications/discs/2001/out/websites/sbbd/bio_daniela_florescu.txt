Biografia Daniela Florescu

Daniela Florescu received her Ph.D. in 1996, on "Search Spaces for 
Object Oriented Query Optimization". She is now a researcher at INRIA 
Rocquencourt, in the Caravel project. Dr. Florescu is among the 
authors of the XML-QL query language and the main designer of 
the Strudel web-site management system. Her current research interests 
include XML technologies (query languages, storage, query optimization), 
static query optimization, data-intensive web site management, 
and data cleaning. Daniela Florescu is also a member of the W3C 
working group on new proposal submitted to the W3C working group.
