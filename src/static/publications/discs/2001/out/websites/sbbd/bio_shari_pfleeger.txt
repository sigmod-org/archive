Biografia Shari Pfleeger

His career in computing has been spent at the interface between research
and application. He has crossed the industry/academic fence several times
with 15 years in IBM, another 15 at Manchester University, almost 3 years
at Harlequin. Since August 1999, He is a Professor of Computing Science at
the University of Newcastle. His Researches Areas are Dependability of
Computer-Based Systems, Concurrency, Logics (for Partial Functions),
(Formal Methods) Support Systems and Other (including VDM).
