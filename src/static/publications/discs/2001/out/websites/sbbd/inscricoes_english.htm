General Information

  • The Brazilian Symposiums of Engineering and Data bases of Software demand individual Register Sessions. It has a discounting for who if it inscribes in the two symposiums. It consults the table of prices.
  • To enroll itself in the tutorial minicourses and of a symposium the Register Session in the corresponding symposium is necessary. Each tutorial minicourse or has its independent price of the price of the symposium.
  • Partners of the SBC - Brazilian Society of Computation has right to a discounting! It must be observed, however, the stated periods of lack stipulated by the proper SBC so that the partner pass to have right in its promoted events. To have right to the partner discounting you must associate until day 31 of August of 2000 . Http://www.sbc.org.br/cadastro/fsocio.html is inquired on the procedures of association in the site http://www.sbc.org.br/cadastro/fsocio.html.
  • Payment mode: deposit in banking account (it sees details in the section payment).
  • Theregister in workshops does not demand the Register Session in one of the symposium.
  • The register in a Symposium includes:
    • All the lectures and sessions techniques of the Symposium
    • Material of the Symposium (book with about 500 pages that registers all the lectures of the symposium).
    • Official program of the Symposium with programming, schedules and other information
    • Shirt of the Symposium
    • Certifyd of the participation
    • Coquetel Aperture
    • Discounting in bars, restaurants and other places during the Symposium.
  • The register in a Minicourse or Tutorial includes:
    • Material of the Minicourse/Tutorial (material written on the mini-curso/tutorial with about 40 pages).
