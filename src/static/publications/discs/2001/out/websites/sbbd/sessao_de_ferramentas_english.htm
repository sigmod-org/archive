  SBES'2000 - Tools Session
   
 

XIV Brazilian Symposium on Software Engineering
Promoted by SBC, Brazilian Computer Society

Joao Pessoa -- Paraiba -- Brazil
October 4 - 6, 2000

The XIV Brazilian Symposium on Software Engineering - SBES'2000 will take place at João Pessoa, eastest point of Brazil, and has been organized by Centro Federal de Educação Tecnológica da Paraíba - CEFET/PB. One of activities of SBES'2000 is the Tools Presentation Session that aims divulge tools for Software Engineering. As already it occurs to some years, one of the activities of XIV the SBES will be the Session of Tools, whose objective is to divulge tools for developed Engineering of Software in our universities, companies or centers of research.

Below the accepted tools for the Session of Tools of XIV the SBES are listed:
