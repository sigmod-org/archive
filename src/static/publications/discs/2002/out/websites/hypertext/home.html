Keynotes

The Hypertext 2001 conference featured keynotes by

  • Paul Kahn is known for his work in Hypertext as the Director of Brown University's Institute for Research in Information and Scholarship (IRIS), and as a President of DynamicDiagrams. Now he is an independent teacher, writer, and information architecture consultant in Paris.
  • Wendy Hall is a well known hypermedia researcher and head of the IAM Research Group at Southampton University, U.K.
