Travel

The official airline of Hypertext 2001 is SAS. If you mention our travel code when making your travel reservations, you can receive discounted fares on SAS flights to and from the conference.

The travel code is DK0114 - you can make travel arrangements at the official SAS conference site.

Airports

  • Århus Airport
