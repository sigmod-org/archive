ICDM '01 Submission Instructions


Papers Due: June 15, 2001

All submissions should be limited to a maximum of 6,000 words (approximately 20 A4 pages). Each submission must contain a short abstract and a complete list of authors (names, affiliations, postal and e-mail addresses, and a fax number of the corresponding author).

Electronic submissions are required in the form of PDF or postscript files. There are no specific format requirements for paper submissions. The IEEE Computer Society Press will send typesetting instructions to authors of accepted papers for the conference proceedings.

The submission deadline has passed. We received 366 paper submissions this year.
