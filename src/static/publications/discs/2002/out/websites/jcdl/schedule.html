Tuesday, 6-10pm

  Banquet and Reception at Virginia Museum of Transportation

Buffet dinner with Virginia wine bar; music by The Celtibillies. Presentation of Vannevar Bush Award around 21:00. Buses leave from Hotel Roanoke main entrance beginning at 18:00, and will be available for transport and return throughout the evening.
Direction for walkers: VMT is about five blocks from HRCC. Take the pedestrian bridge over the railroad tracks, then turn back toward the tracks. Follow the Railwalk west (left when facing the tracks) to Warehouse Row. Pass warehouses on their left side (away from tracks), continue along the row through the parking lot, and pass under the 2nd Street bridge at the Mercury / Redstone rocket. The Virginia Museum of Transportation is the long low brick building directly in front of you. Go to the left of the building; the main entrance is about half way down.
