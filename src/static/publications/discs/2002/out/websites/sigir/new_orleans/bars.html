Bars

Funkybutt at the Congo Square
This Jazz and Blues, club, reminiscent of the great music clubs of the '20's and '30's is located three blocks from Bourbon Street. It also serves creole cuisine in a swanky art deco atmosphere. The club overlooks Armstrong Park, a former gathering place for African slaves, Red Indians, and Creoles.

Le Bon Temps Roule
Known as the "greatest neighborhood bar", Le Bon Temps Roule offers a vast selection of imported beer and savory bar snacks. During the week one can enjoy live music with no cover charge. On Friday nights, raw oysters are served.

The Mermaid Lounge
Located not too far from the French Quarter, the Mermaid Lounge offers a dark and pleasant atmosphere. Local muscians of different genres perform here regularly Check out this site for upcoming events.

Nitebeat
This local publication is a great guide to nightlife in New Orleans. Check out the "Hotspots" and sign the guestbook.

Tipitina's
Formerly a stable, gymnasium, seafood house and infamous brothel, it is now a concert hall. Local and international artists from all musical genres play at Tipitina's.
