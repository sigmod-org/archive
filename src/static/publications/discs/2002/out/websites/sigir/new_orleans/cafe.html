Cafes

Cafe Degas
This cafe is named after the French impressionist, Edgar Degas, who once lived in the nearby neighborhood. It serves French and Creole cuisine. Guests can enjoy indoor and outdoor dining.

Cafe Du Monde
Enjoy Cafe au Lait and Beignets at the original New Orleans French Market Coffee stand. Cafe Du Monde was established in 1862 and is open 24 hours a day seven days a week. Located not too far from the Moon Walk, the Cafe is is a final stopping place for those completing their pub crawls.

Cafe Giovanni
This cafe is located at 117 Rue Decatur. Enjoy the courtyard and Italian cuisine. Guests can choose from oysters, mussels, calamari, escargots, and a variety of exquisite pasta dishes.

Cafe Pontalba
Located on Jackson Square, in the heart of the French Quarter, right next to St. Louis Cathedral. This casual cafe offers a pleasant atmosphere and traditional New Orleans cuisine.

Cafe Rue Bourbon
Guests have enjoyed fine dining at Cafe Rue Bourbon since 1831. The menu consists of traditional New Orleans and French culinary delights. Visit the "Blue Napoleon Wine Room".

Feelings Cafe
Guests can enjoy the ambiance of the piano bar or courtyard dining. Try Oysters en Brochette, Soft Shell Crawfish and Red Snapper Moutarde. Feelings Cafe also has a selection of coffees.

Mimi's Cafe
Choose from a variety of tasty foods: soups, salads, burgers, pasta, and roasted meats. There's also breakfast and a fitness menu. Check out the wine and beer list.

Royal Cafe
Experience balcony dining on the corner of Royal and St. Peter Street in the heart of the French Quarter. Try a hot muffaletta, "Trout LaBranche" and other fine New Orleans' specialties.

Vic's Kangaroo Cafe
Vic's is a charming Australian pub located in the historic warehouse district of New Orleans. Guest can enjoy live R & B music and choose from 14 different beers on tap.
