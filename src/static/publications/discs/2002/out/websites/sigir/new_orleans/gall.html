Galleries


Bergen Galleries
This gallerie specializes in vintage Jazz Fest posters and the secondary market. Some features are Louisiana Art, Mardi Gras and Black Art. Visit this site to see what artists are showing.

Feminine Products No Strings Attached
Here's some art of the sisters, for the sisters. View works made for women, by women. Reviews reveal all......

Fine Arts Gallery of New Orleans
This gallery specializes in fine paintings and sculptures from the 17th to the 20th centuries.

Gallerie De Bautte
This functional art gallery is located at 434 Julia Street in New Orleans. All work is customed designed by Dennis De Bautte. Here you can find coffee tables, dining tables, wine racks, lights, and most important computer tables!

Galerie Royal
feaures originals and graphics by Ronald Jones and Verna Hart. Visit this site for information on local artists and recent showings.

Galerie Simonne Stern
Established in 1967, this gallery specializes in contemporary fine art. Local and regional artists are represented. Visit this web site for information on current exhibitions.

LMS Fine Art and Antiquies
This gallery stocks the largest selection of Russian art in the South.

Mario Villa
This artist-anthropologist hail from Nicaragua. Some of his clientel include Kevin Costner,Carol Burnett, Joanne Woodward, Donald Trump, Princess Caroline of Monaco, Princess Diane de Bourbon of Paris, and Karl Lagerfield of Chanel. Visit the gallery of one of the world's top 100 designers.

Posotive Space
This gallery is dedicated to the art and artists of New Orleans.

Royal Cameo Glass
This gallery specializes in hand blown Art glass. Contemporary artists and those before them are represented.

Southern Lights Studio
The studio maintains an extensive stock collection. The collection contains images of the American South with a concentration in Louisiana and New Orleans. Some work is available from foreign countries including Italy, Mexico, France, Viet Nam and Ireland. Images can be obtained either as film or on disk or via web transmission. Stock sales are handled directly through the studio.

Stone and Press
This gallery features original prints (lithographs, etchings,woodengravings) and drawings by American scene artists of early 20th century plus modern masters of the mezzotint.
