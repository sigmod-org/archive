Louisiana State Museum
This museum is devoted to the historical and cultural hertitage of Louisiana. Visit this site to see the museum's collection of paintings. One can find links to other online exhibits!

New Orleans Museum of Art
The New Orleans Museum of Art is located in City Park. Visit this site for a schedule of current and upcoming exhibits. One can also find information about the museum's collection.

Museums in New Orleans
This page is a comprehensive list of all the museums in New Orleans. Addresses and telephone numbers are included.

Pitot House Museum
Located on Bayou St. John, this 18th century plantation house is within walking distance of the New Orleans Museum of Art.
