Parks


Jean Lafitte National Park
This site contains photos of wildlife found in the Jean Lafitte National park.

Jean Lafitte National Historical Park and Preserve
Formerly the scene of the 1815 Battle of New Orleans, this park features trails and canoe tours. One can view exhibits, films, demonstrations, or take part in an interpretive tour, and go for walks.

New Orleans City Park
Take a stroll under the magnificent oak trees or visit the Museum of Art. This park was once the home of Indians who traded along Bayou St. John. It was also the designated dueling spot of Creole gentlemen until 1890.

New Orleans Jazz National Historical Park
Visit this park and experience the sights, sounds, and places where jazz evolved.

Off to the Parks
This site provides information about Audubon Park, Woldenberg Park, Wilderness Park, Audubon Zoo, the Aquarium of the Americas, and the Entergy IMAX Theatre.
