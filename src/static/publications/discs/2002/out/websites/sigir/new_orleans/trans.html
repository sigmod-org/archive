Air

US Airways and Delta have been selected as official carriers for SIGIR 2001, and are offering discounted fares. Both US Airways and Delta offer a 10% discount on unrestricted round-trip coach fares and a 5% discount on certain other fares.

  • For Delta reservations, you or your travel agent can call Delta Meeting Network Reservations at 1-800-241-6760 and refer to File Number 172531A. See http://www.acm.org/sig_volunteer_info/delta.html
    for more information.

  • For US Airways, call US Airways' Group and Meeting Reservation Office toll free (877)874-7687 and refer to Gold File # 55661791. See http://www.acm.org/sig_volunteer_info/usair.html
    for more information.

The New Orleans International Airport web site has a wide arrary of information about travel to, from and within the city (e.g. cabs, shuttles). In addition, it is served by most major airlines. This site also has links to hotel and other New Orleans information (just click on the "links" link on the left hand menu in the airport site).

New Orleans can be reached by Amtrak train, recalling the famous song celebrating the "City of New Orleans" train from Chicago down to New Orleans. By car, New Orleans is reached via the interstate I-10 highway.

Ground

Dollar Rent a Car
Reserve your car online and receive a %10 discount. Other benefits include frequent flyer miles on American Airlines, America West Airlines, Continental Airlines, Delta, TWA, and United Airlines.

Maps and Transportation
This site provides information about taxi cab services, airport shuttles, public buses, the streetcar, and parking.

Virtual Voyages
This site provides an alphabetical list of all auto rental services within New Orleans. There are links to the services which have web sites. Reservations can be made at some of these sites.
