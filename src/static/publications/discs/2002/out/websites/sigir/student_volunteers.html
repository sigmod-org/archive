Student Volunteers

Sorry--all slots have been filled!

The local arrangements committee needs students to volunteer to work during the SIG/IR conference. The responsibilities of student volunteers would include working the registration desk during the conference, setting up for events, and providing general
assistance to the conference coordinators. In return, the $150 registration fee for the conference will be waived. The student will be able to attend all conference events (which include a poster session, product demonstrations, paper presentations,
and a banquet on Tuesday evening) and will receive a copy of the proceedings.

This is an excellent opportunity for students to attend the conference of an international professional society. If you are interested, contact Dr. Carol Barry at lsbary@lsu.edu.
