Hotel
The Symposium will be held at the Crowne Plaza hotel. A block of rooms has been reserved in the hotel at the special rate of $130 per day, single or double. The hotel reservation deadline has been extended to June 20th, 2001.

To reserve a room, call the hotel at 310-318-8888 or 800-368-9760 and mention that you request the special rate for "SSTD'01" or "University of California, Riverside".

If you are interested in sharing a room, click here.

Travel Information
Shuttle and Taxi
Super Shuttle rate from LAX to the hotel is $9/person. The shuttles stop in front of each terminal. Several other shuttle services are available. For two or more people, a taxi could be cheaper
Driving directions from LAX
Take Sepulveda Blvd South. Continue for about 6 miles (you will pass through El Segundo, Manhattan Beach, and Hermosa Beach). Turn right on Beryl Street, then left on North Harbor Drive. The hotel will be on your left.
Driving directions from elsewhere
From wherever you begin your trip, get to the 405 freeway in Los Angeles. From the 405 freeway, exit at Artesia Blvd. On Artesia go west for approximately 3 miles, then turn left into Sepulveda Blvd (Pacific Coast Highway), Continue south for about 2 miles. In Redondo Beach, turn right on Beryl Street, then left on North Harbor Drive. The hotel will be on your left.
