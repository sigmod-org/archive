Constraint Databases and Temporal Reasoning

Peter Revesz

Department of Computer Science and Engineering
University of Nebraska-Lincoln

Constraint databases provide a general approach to express and solve
constraint problems.  Temporal constraint problems are an important
subclass of applications.  We review constraint databases and
constraint query languages and show that several well-known temporal
and spatio-temporal databases and query languages can be viewed as
subcases.  We also describe the relationship of timed automata and
constraint databases.  Several constraint database systems that can
handle spatio-temporal data will be described, in particular, their
graphical user interface, special built-in operators for data
visualization and animation, indexing, query optimization, and
performance.  The issue of data interoperability among several models
will be also covered.  Finally, we also discuss briefly the trade-off
of greater expressive power and computational complexity.
