VLDB 2001 Book Exhibition

There are two ways for publishers to exhibit and sell books during the conference:

  • Become a sponsor for the conference.  This requires a minium of $10K (at the sponsor level), and gives a host of benefits in addition to the book tables (for a complete list, please look at sponsorship information for VLDB 2001).
  • Get exhibition space, with the fees specified in the exhibition information for VLDB 2001)
We would also appreciate if publishers can gift unsold exhibit books to libraries/students at the end of the conference.

Should you require further information, or wish to request a book exhibition stand, please contact the book exhibition coordinator,  Maria Francesca Costabile .
