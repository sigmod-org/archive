Official Carrier


ALITALIA is the Official Carrier
Foreign delegates may contact their nearest ALITALIA Office (see the addresses and the phone numbers on the Alitalia Web site: http://www.alitalia.it) to request information and make reservations on available flights to ROME (ITALY).
Respond "WQ" when asked for the Identification Code.
Delegates coming from the United States may contact their nearest ALITALIA OFFICE or call the toll-free number 1-800-223-5730 to reserve flights and receive tickets by mail.
All participants will benefit from a special discount on available fares.
