Demonstrations

Selected systems and prototypes developed by research laboratories will be demonstrated in a relatively informal environment. The demonstrations are scheduled to run in the Demo Room, which holds a maximum of 80 delegates thereby providing a suitable atmosphere for interaction between the presenter and the audience. The demonstrations will be repeated to maximize the opportunity to attend. Entrance to the sessions will be on a first come first served basis.
