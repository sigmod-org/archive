REGISTRATION CONDITIONS
• Regular registration includes: attendance at all sessions, copy of proceedings, teas/coffees, lunches, tutorial attendance, admission to Reception, Concert at the Baths of Caracalla and Banquet.
• Student registration includes: everything except the banquet.
• Please submit one registration form for each delegate.
• Fees will be refunded if a written request is received at least 30 days prior to the event. There will be an administrative charge of 20 Euro for cancellations. No refund will be made for cancellations received less than 30 days prior to the event. Substitutions may be made at any time.
• Cancellations will be processed after the conference.

WORKSHOPS

• Cancellations will be processed after the conference.

HOTEL BOOKING CONDITIONS

  1. Rooms will be allocated on a "first come, first served" basis. In the event of your choice being unavailable, we will allocate an alternative room based on the budget of the preferred hotel/proximity to the conference venue.

  2. Reservations will only be accepted using the official accommodation booking form. Any cancellations or amendments must be made through CONSULTA UMBRIA by fax or e-mail and NOT with the hotel.
  3. The booking form must be accompanied by a deposit:
    - 4 star hotel 510.000 ITL / Euro 263,40
    - 3 star hotel 390.000 ITL / Euro 201,42
    - 2 star hotel 264.000 ITL / Euro 136,34
    - Cheap Accomodation 70.000 ITL / Euro 36,16
  4. In the event of non-arrival, the person who has signed the booking form agrees to booking form accepts all legitimate charges raised at the discretion of the hotel.
  5. Hotel Cancellation Charges :
    • 45 days to 30 days prior to arrival : 50% of your total booking charges.
    • 30 days to 15 days or less prior to arrival : 100% of Your total hotel booking charges.
    • Non-arrival : 100% of your total hotel booking charges.
  6. Any additional charges incurred at the hotel must be settled direct with hotel management before departure.
