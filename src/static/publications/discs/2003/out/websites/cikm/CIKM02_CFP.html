ACM CIKM 2002 CALL FOR PAPERS

 

Eleventh International Conference on

 Information and Knowledge Management

 

November 4 -9, 2002

SAIC Headquarters, McLean, Virginia, USA

 

http://www.cikm.org/2002

Sponsored by ACM SIGIR and ACM SIGMIS

 

For the last ten years, CIKM has successfully brought together leading researchers and developers from several areas of information and knowledge management (KM), database management systems (DBMS) and information retrieval (IR).  We continue this tradition of collaboration between information and knowledge management. Over 300 papers were submitted to CIKM in 2001 of which only 25 percent were accepted.  We anticipate similar participation in 2002.  We have a special interest in papers that bridge the areas of databases and information retrieval. Areas of interest include but are not limited to:

 

   Data and Knowledge Sharing

   Data Mining, Knowledge Discovery

   IR Effectiveness and Efficiency

   Integrating Structured and Unstructured Data

   Data Warehouses, OLAP

   Distributed Object Management

   Digital Libraries

   Federated Information Systems

   Heterogeneous and Distributed Systems

   Hypertext and Hypermedia

 

   Semi-Structured Data Management

   Query Processing

   Interactive Data Exploration

   Intelligent Information Systems

   Information Filtering and Summarization

   Information Visualization

   Information Classification

   Intelligent Mediators

   Knowledge Management

   Data Management in Mobile Environments

   Multimedia and Spatial Databases

 

 

Paper Submission Instructions:

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research papers that are not being considered in another forum. At least one author will be required to attend the conference to present the paper. An electronically submitted abstract will be required prior to the submission of the full paper. Manuscripts should contain no more than 5000 words and be printed on 10 or 12 size font. Electronic submission of manuscripts, preferably in PDF format, is required. Submissions should include the paper title, abstract, name of authors, their affiliation, email address, and postal address. In addition, the author responsible for correspondence should include his/her telephone number and complete email address. The papers should be submitted electronically through the CIKM2002 Online Submission System. Detailed instructions for electronic submission will be available at http://www.cikm.org/2002.

Tutorial Proposals

 

Tutorial Submission:

The Program Committee of CIKM 2002 solicits tutorial proposals. Each tutorial will be presented in a 3 hours session, for half-day sessions, on topics of current relevance to information and knowledge management. Tutorial proposals must include title, objective, intended audience, prerequisite, contents to be covered, instructors and their biography.  If you are interested in proposing a tutorial, please send a proposal to the tutorial chair, Dr. Il-Yeol Song at song@drexel.edu.

 

Workshop Submission:

CIKM has been held with several workshops such as GIS, WIDM, DOLAP, and DocEng. If you are interested in organizing a new workshop to be held with CIKM, send your proposal to the workshop chair, Dr. R. Scott Cost at cost@acm.org.

 

Panel Submission:

We also encourage proposals for panels on new and controversial issues related to the conference topics.   Proposals on topics that emphasize the interaction between research areas are particularly welcome.  Panel proposals must be debate-oriented and must pose specific questions that panelists can be called upon to answer. A list of probable participants with their affiliations should be included in the proposal. Panel proposals should be submitted by May 27, 2002.

 

Industry Track Submission:

We solicit high-quality presentations which provide in-depth technical discussion of:

  • information and knowledge management products and marketplace trends
  • requirements of novel applications

§         important research problems from an industrial perspective

  • lessons learned applying research results to real problems

By July 15, 2002 (firm), please submit one of the following to the industry track chair, Len Seligman (seligman@mitre.org):

§         1-3 page abstract (in PDF, postscript, or Word) describing your proposed presentation, or

  • 1-2 paragraph abstract plus draft presentation slides (in Powerpoint, PDF, postscript, or html).

Accepted abstracts will be published in the conference proceedings. In addition, selected abstracts will be considered for publication (as full papers) in the Industry Perspectives section of SIGMOD Record.
