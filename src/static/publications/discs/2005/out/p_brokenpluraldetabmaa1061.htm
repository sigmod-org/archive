Broken plural detection for arabic information retrieval


Abduelbaset Goweder, Massimo Poesio, and Anne N. De Roeck

  View Paper (PDF)  

Return to Posters


Abstract

, the effects of broken plurals on the performance of information retrieval systems has not been examined.We propose a light stemmer that incorporates a broken plural recognition component, and evaluate it within the context of information retrieval. Our results show that identifying broken plurals and reducing them to their correct stems does result in a significant improvement in the performance of information retrieval systems.
