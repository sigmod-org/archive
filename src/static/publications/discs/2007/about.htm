ACM SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection, DiSC, is a comprehensive collection of periodicals in the areas of databases and information management. It also includes videos of SIGMOD/PODS 2007 keynotes, invited talks, and tutorials. All included forums are from 2005, 2006, and 2007.
