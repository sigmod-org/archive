ACM SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection, DiSC, is a comprehensive collection of periodicals in the areas of databases and information management. This is a combined issue of DiSC, DiSC '07/'08, which includes many articles published through conferences, symposiums, workshops, and journals in 2006 and 2007. These materials are published by the ACM, Springer and the VLDB Endowment.
