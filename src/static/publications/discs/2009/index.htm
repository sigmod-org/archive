ACM SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection, DiSC, is a collection of periodicals in the areas of databases and information management. This is a combined issue of DiSC, DiSC '09/'10, which includes many articles published through conferences, symposiums, workshops, and journals in 2008 and 2009. These materials are published by the ACM, IEEE, Australian Computer Society, Brazilian Computer Society, and VLDB Endowment.
