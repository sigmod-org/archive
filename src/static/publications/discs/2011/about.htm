ACM SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection, DiSC, is a collection of periodicals in the areas of databases and information management. This 12th issue of DiSC, DiSC '11, includes many articles published through conferences, symposiums, workshops, and journals. It also includes videos from SIGMOD/PODS 2010. All included forums are from 2010. These materials are published by the ACM, IEEE, the Australian Computer Society, and the VLDB Endowment.

This is the last volume of DiSC. In recent years Springer-Verlag and the IEEE both decided to withdraw permission for material that had appeared in past volumes of DiSC, including ICDE, TKDE, DASFAA, and ER. So we (the ACM SIGMOD Executive Committee) decided to discontinue DiSC.

I hope you enjoy this final volume of DiSC!

Curtis Dyreson
Utah State University
