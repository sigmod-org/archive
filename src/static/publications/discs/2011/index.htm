ACM SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection, DiSC, is a collection of periodicals in the areas of databases and information management. This is the final issue of DiSC, DiSC '11, which includes many articles published through conferences, symposiums, workshops, and journals in 2010. It also includes videos from the 2010 SIGMOD/PODS conference. These materials are published by the ACM, IEEE, Australian Computer Society, and VLDB Endowment.
